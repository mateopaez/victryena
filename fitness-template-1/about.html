Who I Am

Strength, Confidence & Autonomy

I help gender-diverse individuals build strength, confidence, and autonomy through personal training and gender-affirming programming.

Working out can be intimidating, but whether you are cis or trans, everyone deserves to feel at home in their own body. My coaching is built around meeting you exactly where you are — no judgment, no assumptions, just a plan that fits your body and your goals.
